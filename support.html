OdaBoya — Support

OdaBoya repaints the walls in a photo of your room, realistically, so you can choose a color with confidence.

The painted result looks odd

Best results come from a well-lit photo with at least one clearly visible wall, taken straight on. Very dark rooms, mirrors and busy wallpaper can confuse the AI — try another angle or more light.

“Daily painting limit” message

Each device gets a generous number of AI paints per day to keep the service free. The counter resets within 24 hours.

My gallery is empty

The gallery fills up automatically after your first successful paint. If it stays empty, check your internet connection.

How do I delete a painted room?

Gallery → tap the image → Delete. It is removed from cloud storage immediately.
